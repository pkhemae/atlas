# Ambient sound attributions

## rain.m4a

Derived from [Calm rain.wav](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Calm_rain.wav)
by **Zuvji**, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Modifications (this derivative is likewise distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0):

- Trimmed to a 70-second segment (100 s – 174 s of the original).
- Made seamlessly loopable with a 4-second equal-power crossfade
  (the tail of the segment fades into its head).
- Padded with 2-second margins on both sides so playback can loop the
  exact 70 s region (`loopStart = 2 s`, `loopEnd = 72 s`) regardless of
  codec edge padding.
- Re-encoded to AAC (128 kbps, 44.1 kHz stereo).
